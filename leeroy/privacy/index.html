Last updated: 30 September 2026. App: Leeroy! (dev.level82.leeroy) by LEVEL82 (level82.dev).

In short

Leeroy! does not collect, share or sell any personal data: it sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (stars, best scores, last plans), the name you type for your character, your language and the sound setting on your device. We never receive this data.

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android itself may include this data in your backup and restore it on a new phone or after you reinstall the game. That backup belongs to you: you turn it on or off and delete it in your device settings, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the copy on the device.

Permissions

You are never asked to grant a permission. Android lists one for the app, network access, because the framework the game is built on declares it by default; Leeroy! never uses it: everything it needs is inside the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. Questions: LEVEL82, level82.dev.
